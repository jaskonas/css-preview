**David Hendrickson**, Professor of International Relations, Colorado College, and Visiting Senior Fellow, CSS

“The Law of Nature and International Relations”

CSS Visiting Senior Fellow and Colorado College professor David Hendrickson discusses the ideas of Protestant jurists of the 17th and 18th centuries, including Grotius, Pufendorf, Vattel, and the framers of the US Constitution, and examines the relevance of their thinking to international relations today.
