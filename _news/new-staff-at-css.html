The Center for the Study of Statesmanship is very pleased to announce that Justin T. Logan has been appointed Director of Programs and Research Associate at the Center for the Study of Statesmanship.

Justin is a Magna Cum Laude graduate of American University and holds a Masters Degree in International Relations from the University of Chicago. He previously served as Director of Foreign Policy Studies at the Cato Institute here in Washington. In his new role, Justin will help plan and manage CSS’s programming. He will also conduct research and write on international relations topics. We are delighted to welcome Justin to the CSS team.
