Founding Director Claes G. Ryn’s A Common Human Ground: Universality and Particularity in a Multicultural World is out now with new new preface from the University of Missouri Press. In this book, Professor Ryn sets forth a philosophy of human interaction that he applies to foreign policy and international relations.

The book is available for purchase now.
