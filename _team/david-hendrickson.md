David Hendrickson is professor of political science at Colorado College, where he has taught since 1983, and was chair of the Political Science department from 2000 to 2003. He teaches courses in American foreign policy and international relations. Hendrickson is the author most recently of [*Republic in Peril: American Empire and the Liberal Tradition*](https://www.amazon.com/Republic-Peril-American-Liberal-Tradition/dp/0190660384/) (Oxford University Press, 2017), as well as seven other books. Hendrickson holds a Ph.D. in Political Science from Johns Hopkins University and a BA in History from Colorado College.
