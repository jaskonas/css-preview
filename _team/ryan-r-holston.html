Ryan R. Holston is Professor and holder of the Jonathan Myric Daniels ’61 Chair for Academic Excellence at Virginia Military Institute. He is also an Associate Editor at the journal Humanitas. His published work has appeared in History of Political Thought, Telos, and Harvard Theological Review, among other places. He is currently writing a monograph, whose working title is Tradition and the Deliberative Turn, and is co-editor of a forthcoming book entitled The Historical Mind: Humanistic Renewal in a Post-Constitutional Age (Albany, NY: SUNY Press, 2020).
